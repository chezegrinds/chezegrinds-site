Frequently asked

Summer Grinds FAQ

When do Summer Grinds run?

From late June through August. We schedule around holidays — pause and restart as needed.

Which subjects and levels?

Maths, Physics and Chemistry. Junior Cycle and Leaving Cert. Higher and Ordinary Level.

Online or in person?

Online via a private Zoom room. Same focus, none of the travel — your child can work from anywhere.

How is it different from group grinds?

Group grinds cover the syllabus. 1 to 1 covers what your child specifically needs — gaps, weak topics, exam technique, confidence. Everything is tailored.

How does payment work?

Plans start from €110 every 4 weeks. If a weekly payment plan suits the family better, we'll split it that way — no extra cost. We agree the exact package and schedule on the discovery call.

What if it's not the right fit?

No long lock-ins. If the tutor isn't clicking, we re-match. If Summer Grinds isn't what you need, just stop after the month.
